# Marketplace Listing Draft

## Universal Matching Platform

A configurable, commercially oriented matching-platform source product for building services such as dating, friendship, business networking, mentorship, communities and custom matching categories.

### What is included

- Web, Admin/API and mobile source components
- configurable onboarding and profile schemas
- discovery and matching foundations
- mutual matching and direct messaging
- international and regional configuration
- safety and moderation foundations
- configurable feature and terminology settings
- Admin Quick Launch workflow
- immutable published configuration history
- installation, deployment and customization documentation

### Ideal for

Buyers who want to launch a matching-oriented service quickly, customize an existing foundation, or use the platform as a base for a specialized product.

### Before purchase

This is a source-product foundation, not a managed hosting service. Buyers should evaluate infrastructure, provider, legal and operational requirements before launch.

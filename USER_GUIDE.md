# User Guide

## Purpose

This guide describes the standard member journey. Individual deployments may rename concepts through Quick Launch terminology and may enable different categories or profile fields.

## Typical journey

1. Create or sign in to an account.
2. Complete the profile fields required by the deployment.
3. Browse discovery candidates in enabled categories.
4. Record a like or pass decision.
5. When interest is mutual, open the resulting conversation.
6. Send and read durable messages.
7. Update the profile as allowed by the deployment.

## Privacy and safety

Use only information you are comfortable sharing according to the deployment's privacy policy. Report suspicious behavior through the deployment's supported safety or moderation channels.

## Important behavior

Matching and authorization decisions are server-authoritative. Client presentation settings do not grant additional privileges. Availability of features can differ between deployments.

For mobile-specific acceptance behavior, see MOBILE_ACCEPTANCE_CHECKLIST.md.

# Support Scope

## Included documentation support

The distributed package documents installation, Quick Launch configuration, deployment boundaries, customization contracts and troubleshooting.

## Buyer-operated responsibilities

Unless a separate commercial agreement explicitly says otherwise, the buyer is responsible for:

- hosting and infrastructure
- cloud/provider accounts and billing
- domains and TLS
- production secrets
- legal and regulatory compliance
- moderation and user operations
- data retention and backups
- custom development
- third-party provider outages

## Support boundary

The source product is designed to be self-hosted and customized. Do not represent the package as including ongoing managed operations, legal compliance services or unlimited custom development.

Follow SECURITY.md for responsible vulnerability handling.
